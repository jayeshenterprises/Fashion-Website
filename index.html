Our Story

A curated fashion destination built with love, for every Indian woman.

Who We Are
Fashion That Inspires

SheStyles started with one simple idea — Indian women deserve a fashion space that truly understands them. Not just trends, but real outfits for real life. College mornings, café afternoons, festive evenings, and everything in between.

Every single piece on SheStyles is handpicked. We spend hours finding outfits that are stylish, affordable, and actually wearable — so you don't have to scroll endlessly through hundreds of options.

From effortless casuals to festive elegance — we curate outfits that make you feel confident, comfortable, and completely yourself.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Welcome to SheStyles

We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how our website works. Please read this policy carefully.

Affiliate Links

SheStyles uses affiliate links from platforms like Myntra, Amazon, and Flipkart. When you click a product link on our website and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. We only recommend products we genuinely believe in and that we think will be a good fit for our audience.

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information directly on this website. No registration or login is required to browse or shop. We do not use tracking cookies or analytics on our end. If you fill out our contact form, that information is handled by Google Forms and subject to Google's privacy policy.

Third-Party Links

Our website contains links to third-party platforms including Myntra, Amazon, Flipkart, and others. Once you click through to these platforms, their own privacy policies apply. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external websites.

Pinterest

We maintain the account @shestylesofficial on Pinterest. Any interaction you have on Pinterest is subject to Pinterest's own privacy policy and terms of service. We use Pinterest to share fashion inspiration and link back to our curated collection.

Cookies

Our website itself does not use cookies. Third-party platforms you visit through our affiliate links may use their own cookies as per their policies.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or how we operate, please reach out through our contact page. We're happy to help.
